Boids

About Project
This project is a game built for the Nitendo Switch where the player must retrieve their "Moolons" from the evil melon enemies. The player, "Ela", has the power of the Elasticband, able to whip her enemies, slingshot herself to local islands and lift large crates to transverse the mesa and collect her herd.
Project Details
  • Unity Engine
  • Nintendo Switch
  • C#
  • Level Design
  • Mechanics Programmer
Pulley Mechanic
A main area of focus on this project was designing and developing the pulley mechanic, where the player would be able lift crates blocking paths or raise crates to create platforms for the player to cross. I further developed this by introducing classes for "fences" to attach the crate height as a fixed possition as well as using Lerps to ease the movement of the crates fall.
Melon Mesa 1
My other area of focus was in development of the first level of the game, introducing core mechanics and the main objective the player would have to undergo. I had three "Moolons" for the player to collect across the level, all requiring a different implementation of the pulley mechanic such as platforms, barriers or bridges. The level also consists of Melon Enemies gradually increasing in difficulty class fpr the player to learn attack methods.
